# Startup Crucible — Track 3 Hackathon Project

## Context
I am a founder in SF. I am attending a multiplayer ai hackathon in person at SF. 

## The track that I am going to submit into

​Track 3: Grow with Agents

​The roles are reversed. Usually humans train AI; here, agents train humans. Agents step into expert roles such as interviewer, examiner, professor, coach, boss or client, and the human learns, practises, gets tested and improves through a visual, interactive simulation.

​What this track involves:

​Agents play expert roles such as interviewer, examiner, coach or boss, as visible characters

​At least 2–3 agents coordinate about the human

​Agents adapt to how the human is performing

​The session ends with a clear result and evidence-based feedback

​Examples: A 3D interview panel that questions you, deliberates and gives a verdict. An office simulator with an agent boss, teammates and a difficult client. A driving test with an agent examiner in a simulated city. A sales call where you pitch to a buying committee that pushes back on every claim.

## My idea
A web based 3d boardroom with investor and founder avatars/personas similar to peter thiel, paul graham, garry tan, vinod khosla, etc. I dont think I am allowed to use their likeness, so we can just use their personas like the monopolist strategist, the user pull purist, etc. The user and the avatars talk through the user's or an idea generated by an avatar to talk about startup idea or societal problem.  

## Hackathon Constraints and requirements
You must follow these constraints or else my project will be rejected
- Characters: Each agent has a visual identity: 3D model
- Visual output: An animated video, a running simulation or a playable game
- Movement & action: Characters move, react and act on screen, not just output text
- 3+ agents: At least three agents with their own goals, roles or personalities
-Decision log: A visible log or ledger of what the agents decided and why
- Bruin is mandatory for all projects. (The open source ai data platform from getbruin.com)

## Hackathon Score Boosters

- 3D: 2D is welcome, but 3D is encouraged and scores higher
- ML & trained agents: RL, fine-tuning, learned behaviour or memory that changes decisions
- Voice: Agents speak with their own voices

## Common pitfalls

ALL TRACKS
Text-only agents
✕ Agents whose decisions only show up as chat messages.
✓ Every decision becomes movement or action on screen.

Invisible reasoning
✕ Things happen and nobody knows why.
✓ A decision log: "Rex betrayed the crew because he was promised the pod."

Vibes-only feedback
✕ "Great job! 8/10."
✓ Quote what the human said and tie every score to evidence from the session.
